Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Career Connect! We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is managed in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use,and safeguard your when you use our placement services for college students.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: This includes your name, address, phone number, email address, date of birth, and other contact details you provide when you register, create a profile, or interact with our services.
  • Educational Information: Details about your educational background, such as school/college name, degree, major, GPA, transcripts, and graduation date.
  • Employment Information: Information about your work experience, including internships, job titles, employers, responsibilities, and references.
  • Technical Information: Information collected automatically when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, browsing activity, and cookies.
  • Other Information: Any other information you choose to provide, such as resumes, cover letters, and other documents relevant to your job search.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide and manage our placement services, including matching you with potential employers and facilitating job applications.
  • Communication: To contact you regarding job opportunities, application statuses, and to send newsletters or promotional materials if you have opted in.
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your information with:

  • Employers: Potential employers to facilitate the placement process.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party companies that help us operate our business, such as IT service providers, data storage, and customer support.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, to comply with a legal obligation, protect and defend our rights or property, or protect the personal safety of our users or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred to the new owner.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal obligations.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Data Portability: Request transfer of your personal information to another service provider.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. You can control the use of cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website and updating the effective date.
